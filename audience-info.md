My audience
Who: non-resident entrepreneurs (especially UAE-based), e-commerce founders, consultants, and freelancers building an international business
Main problem: they want to launch fast but feel confused about US vs UK setup, compliance, banking, and tax implications, and they are afraid of making costly mistakes
Goal: form a US LLC or UK LTD remotely, with a reliable process, so they can invoice clients, receive payments, and grow confidently
Language they use: “I don’t know which structure is right for me”, “Can I open this without traveling?”, “What’s the real total cost?”, “Will I actually get a business bank account?”, “I want zero surprises”
Results they want: launch their company in days (not months), get key documents fast (EIN/UTR depending on jurisdiction), open a business bank account, stay compliant, and start closing clients quickly
